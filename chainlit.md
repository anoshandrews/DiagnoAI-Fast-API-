# ⚕️ DiagnoAI: Clinical Symptom Intake

Welcome to **DiagnoAI**, an agentic symptom intake assistant powered by **Stateful LangGraph** and **Groq**.

### 🌟 What DiagnoAI Does:
* 🩺 **Structured Intake**: Systematically gathers your symptoms following clinical **SOCRATES / OPQRST** guidelines (onset, duration, location, severity, triggers).
* 🚨 **Red Flag Guardrails**: Instantly detects acute emergency signs (crushing chest pain, severe shortness of breath, stroke signs) and directs you to immediate emergency care.
* 🎙️ **Voice Enabled**: Speak or attach audio recordings transcribed with ultra-low latency via **Groq Whisper Turbo**.
* 📋 **Clinician Handoff**: Synthesizes a structured **SBAR / SOAP** intake report with differential diagnostic considerations for your physician.
